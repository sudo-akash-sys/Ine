## Finding

A `group` names already-declared entities and never declares them (semantic._expand_groups raises otherwise), so extract-to-group cannot replace the `make` lines it collects; it inserts the group line and collapses only the members' shared literal `inside` line and property lines they hold identically, leaving all other lines byte-identical — the prototype proves entities, properties, relationships and rendered HTML are unchanged. Two gaps for any real action: statements carry only a 1-based `line` (no column/offset/source mapping), and every precondition must be checked against the raw parse because analyze() reports post-expansion statements that may correspond to no line in the buffer.
